A visual prototype and build spec for adding Apple CarPlay and Android Auto to Radio Theater. Drive the mock head unit below, then hand Cursor the exact instructions at the bottom of this page.

⚠️

Do this first — before any code: CarPlay needs Apple’s CarPlay Audio entitlement. Apply in the Apple Developer portal (Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles → request the CarPlay entitlement for the app ID) and in App Store Connect. Approval runs on Apple’s clock. Android Auto has no equivalent gate — it uses the existing Media3 service and a automotive_app_desc.xml declaration.

02 — What the driver does

Four flows, nothing more

If a flow needs more than these steps, cut it. The car is not the place for the phone app’s full feature set.

Flow A — Resume

App opens to Home → “Continue Playing” resumes the last episode at its saved position in one tap. Zero browsing. Same thing across devices: listen on the phone, get in the truck, and Home opens with that episode's year selected and its row highlighted dark — tap it and it resumes where the phone left off.

Flow B — Browse

Home → tap a year in the 3×3 grid (1974–1982) → the episode list beside it updates in place → tap an episode (or its play triangle) → plays. One screen, no pushing at all. (Library → Years remains as the list-based route.)

Flow C — Favorites / Downloaded

On Home, flip the All Shows / Favorites toggle to Favorites → the year grid disappears and the right panel lists only your favorite episodes → tap one → plays. Favoriting itself is phone-only; the car only views the list. Downloaded is reached from Library → Downloaded and works with no signal — this is the road-trip case.

Flow D — Voice

“Play Radio Theater, 1977” / “Resume Radio Theater” via Siri or Google Assistant through the media session. No in-car search screen.

03 — Screen-by-screen spec

Every screen Cursor must build

Same content on both platforms; only the template / component names differ. Structure and labels deliberately mirror Apple Podcasts in CarPlay (Listen Now / Library / Browse / Playing Next) so the app feels familiar on first use. And one layout rule everywhere: no tab bar and no in-content side menu — the Home screen is the navigation, and the platform's own rail already occupies the left side.

CarPlay + Auto: two-pane Home layout (brand lockup + year grid | episode list)

1. Home — the main screen

Scott's home-screen design, and the screen the app opens to: a two-pane split. Left pane (~55%, black): the brand lockup — a small vintage-microphone mark beside “RADIO THEATER” in large gold serif capitals, with “Broadcast Recordings of CBS Radio Mystery Theater” in smaller gold beneath it — then the All Shows / Favorites toggle (below), over a 3×3 grid of large year buttons, 1974–1982. The selected year is a filled white button with black text; unselected years are dark with a light outline and light text. Right pane (~45%): the selected year's episode list — each episode's real artwork thumbnail with its 4-digit episode number (0001, 0002, …) beneath it, the title in bold, a thin listening-progress bar under the title, and a short synopsis; at the row's right edge, a play triangle and Copy to Phone. Tapping a year updates the list beside it — no screen change. Tapping a row or its play triangle opens Now Playing for that episode.

  • All Shows / Favorites toggle: a centered toggle row sits in the left pane between the brand lockup and the year grid — the label “All Shows”, a pill-shaped switch with a sliding knob, and the label “Favorites”. The labels use the same font as the year-button text. The active label is full white (100%); the inactive label is dimmed to 50% white — the same dimming as the unselected year buttons' text and outlines. Tapping either label, or the switch itself, selects that side, and the knob animates between the two positions. All Shows (the default, and how the demo opens) is exactly the Home described above: year grid visible, right panel showing the selected year's episodes. Favorites: the year grid disappears entirely — the grid area goes empty/black while the logo and toggle stay — and the right panel lists only the user's favorite episodes, in the same row format as a year list (artwork, number, title, progress, synopsis, play, Copy to Phone); the currently playing episode's row keeps its dark charcoal fill there too. Tapping a favorite row or its play triangle opens Now Playing, same as a year-list row. Switching back to All Shows restores the year grid with the previously selected year and its episode list, untouched. This toggle is how Favorites are reached in the car: favoriting itself remains phone-only — the car only views the list
  • Copy to Phone = save for offline: it saves that episode onto the phone for offline playback — the phone app's existing download feature, triggered from the car. Users know it from the phone app: before a long road trip without a phone connection, they save episodes onto the phone. The checkbox shows and controls whether that episode is saved on the phone, and it uses the same download store as the phone app
  • Scrolling: the episode list scrolls by drag gesture while the brand lockup and year grid stay fixed — in the car, that drag-scroll is the platform's system-provided gesture in list views
  • Current episode row: the episode that is currently playing / in progress carries a dark charcoal background fill on its row — clearly distinct from the plain black list background — so the driver can see at a glance which episode is the current one
  • Phone-to-car continuity: playback state is shared between the phone app and the car — the same store as favorites and progress, no separate car-side state. A user listening to an episode on the phone gets in the vehicle and CarPlay / Android Auto comes up with that same episode selected: Home opens with the in-progress episode's year selected in the grid and that episode's row highlighted dark; tapping it opens Now Playing on that episode, resuming playback at the position where they left off on the phone
  • Open item: real CarPlay list templates limit row content (title + subtitle + artwork), so the per-row synopsis and Copy to Phone control are design intent to be reconciled at build time — keep them if the template allows, otherwise use the closest supported form without changing the two-pane layout
  • Gold appears only in the brand lockup; both panes stay in the dark, neutral car theme, and episode artwork is the only other color
CarPlay: CPListTemplate · Auto: MediaLibrary browse node [listen-now]

2. Listen Now — resume

Modeled on Apple Podcasts' Listen Now. Three sections, in order: Continue Playing (in-progress episodes with progress bar + remaining time), Up Next (the current queue), Recently Played. The app opens to the Home screen (card 1); this is where resuming lives.

  • No tab bar: Listen Now · Library · Browse are content groupings in the browse tree, not on-screen tabs — the Home screen replaces the tab structure, and there is no Search or Settings screen in the car
  • Layout rule: no in-content side menu either — the platform's own navigation rail already occupies the left side
CarPlay: CPListTemplate · Auto: browse nodes [years] [saved] [downloaded]

3. Library — three rows

One list, Podcasts-Library style, exactly three rows: Years (opens 1974–1982, then that year's episodes), Saved (the user's favorites — Podcasts' word), Downloaded (offline episodes).

  • Empty states stay as rows: “No saved episodes yet” — never hide a row
  • Android Auto mirrors the same order in its browse tree: Listen Now content first, then Years, Saved, Downloaded
CarPlay: CPListTemplate with featured items · Auto: curation stays on the phone

4. Browse — curated

Modeled on Podcasts' Browse. Two featured entries with artwork: This Week in Theater History (the episode that aired this calendar week in the original run) and Hidden Gems (curated picks), then a short Editor's Picks episode list.

  • Featured entries are computed on the phone from catalog air dates — the car only renders rows
  • Keep it short: 2 featured entries + a handful of picks, never a wall of episodes
CarPlay: CPListTemplate → CPListItem (artwork + text) · Auto: browse nodes [years] / [year/{year}]

5. Years → Episode List

From Library → Years: one row per season year, 1974–1982, chronological, 1974 first. Tapping a year pushes that year's episodes: each episode's own artwork thumbnail, title, air date + runtime as subtitle. Downloaded episodes get a badge.

  • Artwork: Assets/Artwork/Episodes/ep%04d.webp by episode number; paletteHex from catalog.json is the fallback tile color
  • Cap the rendered list (paginate / window) — some cars show as few as 12 rows; never hand the car 200+ rows at once
  • No synopsis text in this list's rows — title + date only (short synopses live in the Home screen's episode list — card 1) · Sort: episode number ascending
CarPlay: CPNowPlayingTemplate · Auto: MediaSession + custom actions

6. Now Playing

Scott's mockup layout: header with a back chevron “‹” at top-left and the app name “Radio Theater” at top-right. Large artwork at RIGHT — that episode's own ep%04d.webp file, roughly the right third of the screen. Background: the background is the episode artwork, blurred and darkened, matching what CarPlay itself does with supplied artwork — the same artwork file fills the whole screen edge to edge (top included), scaled up well past the edges and heavily blurred into a soft wash of its own colors, under a strong black overlay (about 60%) so the screen stays deep and moody and all white text and icons stay legible; it swaps automatically when the episode changes and is one continuous surface with no seams, lines, or bands. Title, host line (“E. G. Marshall”), and show line (“CBS Radio Mystery Theater”) stacked at LEFT and center-aligned within that text area, with the transport centered beneath the text block. The progress bar runs across the BOTTOM: elapsed at far left, remaining (with minus sign) at far right. Reached via the system Now Playing button, never from a menu of ours.

  • Transport: −15s, Play/Pause, +30s (asymmetric, as in the mockup), centered under the text block — rendered as bare white icons directly on the background, no button circles or filled backgrounds, matching the phone app; the skip icons are a curved arrow wrapping around the number (15 / 30)
  • Previous / Next Episode buttons at the bottom corners, beneath the progress bar: skip-to-previous icon + two-line “Previous / Episode” label at far left; “Next / Episode” label + skip-to-next icon at far right. Tapping moves through the episode list and updates artwork, title, blurred artwork background, and progress. The same actions also stay on the steering-wheel controls
  • No swipe: CarPlay and Android Auto templates don't support swipe-to-change-episode — prev/next must be tappable targets, not gestures
  • Speed: turtle / rabbit stepper bottom-center, beneath the progress bar — turtle steps down, rabbit steps up, current rate shown between them (0.75× · 1× · 1.25× · 1.5× · 2×) — the same control as the phone app, drawn the same way: bare white turtle and rabbit icons on the background, no button circles
  • No Save or “Playing Next” pills on the Now Playing face — the mockup has neither. There is no Playing Next / queue screen in the car design; the queue is not surfaced in the car at all, and playback simply continues through the episode list via the Previous/Next Episode controls
  • Saved state must match the phone app instantly
System-provided — do not build a custom one

7. Voice & Lock-Screen Parity

Car, lock screen, and headphones all drive the same media session. If it works on the lock screen, it must work in the car.

  • Support: play, pause, next/prev track, seek −15s/+30s, seek to position
  • Media metadata: title, “CBS Radio Mystery Theater”, year, artwork, duration

screen (step 2) — there is no tab bar. The driver can resume the last
